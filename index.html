Chip Rack

Casino trip & bankroll tracker

In testing — coming to Google Play and the App Store

What it does

You log what you bought in for and what you walked away with. Chip Rack does the rest — per session, per trip, and over time. It's the notes app, the spreadsheet and the back of the envelope, replaced with something built for the job.

Trips & sessions

Group sittings into trips with a starting bankroll, or keep an open-ended tab for routine play near home.

Every game, not just craps

Craps, blackjack, baccarat, roulette, poker, slots, video poker and the house-banked table games — with variants and side bets.

Reports that answer questions

Bankroll over time, profit and loss by casino and by game, and patterns by day of week and time of day. Every chart drills down to the sessions behind it.

Tax-ready records

Generate a win/loss report as CSV or PDF for your own records, whenever you need it.

Your data stays yours

Everything you enter is stored on your device and nowhere else. No account, no sign-in, no server holding your records — and nothing for anyone to sell, because we never receive it. Export a full copy whenever you like. Read the privacy policy →

Not a gambling app

Chip Rack involves no wagering, no real money, no play money, and no games. It doesn't connect to a casino, a sportsbook, or a payment system. It's a ledger for activity that happened somewhere else.

About Inside Craps

Inside Craps builds tools for table players. Chip Rack is the first — a free, game-agnostic record keeper, with deeper craps-specific analytics planned to follow.
